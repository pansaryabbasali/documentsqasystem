ENGINEERING CHANGE NOTICE

ECN No:            ECN-2031
Date issued:        14 May 2026
Status:             Approved
Product line affected: AF-4520 (AF-4500 series)

TITLE
Impeller material change — cast iron to duplex stainless steel

DESCRIPTION OF CHANGE
The AF-4520 impeller material is changed from cast iron (ASTM A48 Class 30) to
duplex stainless steel (ASTM A890 Grade 4A). No change is made to impeller
geometry, hydraulic profile, or mounting dimensions.

REASON FOR CHANGE
Field service records from Q4 2025 through Q1 2026 show a pattern of premature
impeller erosion on AF-4520 units installed in high-chloride water treatment
applications, with several units requiring impeller replacement inside 18 months
against a 5-year design life target. Root cause analysis attributes the wear to
chloride-accelerated erosion-corrosion of the cast iron impeller in these
service conditions. Duplex stainless steel offers substantially higher resistance
to chloride-induced erosion-corrosion at a moderate cost increase.

AFFECTED PART NUMBERS
  Previous: IMP-4520-CI  (cast iron impeller, AF-4520)
  New:      IMP-4520-DSS (duplex stainless steel impeller, AF-4520)

EFFECTIVITY
Applies to all AF-4520 units with serial numbers AF4520-2026-0500 and later.
Units below this serial number range are not affected by this change and
continue to use IMP-4520-CI for spares unless a customer requests an upgrade.

IMPACT ASSESSMENT
  - Mounting dimensions: No change. Fully interchangeable with prior impellers.
  - Weight: Increases by approximately 4 kg due to material density difference.
  - Wear ring clearance: No change to specification (see IOM-AF4500-REV-D, Section 7).
  - Cost: Unit material cost increases by approximately 9% for the impeller assembly.
  - Warranty: No impact to standard warranty terms (POL-WARR-REV-5).
  - Spare parts: Existing IMP-4520-CI stock may continue to be sold for units below
    the effectivity serial number; new orders and upgrade requests use IMP-4520-DSS.

RECOMMENDED ACTION FOR FIELD SERVICE
For any AF-4520 unit in a high-chloride application showing early wear ring or
impeller erosion during scheduled inspection, recommend the customer upgrade to
IMP-4520-DSS at the next planned maintenance window, regardless of original
serial number.
